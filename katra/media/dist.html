Where to put the bound: 488,030 handles, log scale

Handle length as a multiple of its run's arc length. Legitimate fits and blow-ups are cleanly bimodal, so the constant only has to land in the empty space between them.

Both earlier proposals sat at an edge of that gap rather than in it. 1 × arc lands on the legitimate p99 and rejects 900 honest fits; 3 × chord is undefined where the chord vanishes — six corpus runs have a chord of exactly 0.0000px against arcs of 166–210px. On this corpus all three produce equivalent output; the difference is which one has an answer for inputs the corpus does not contain.
